# Masko animation brief — Twist mascot launch

Source still: the **standing** pose (both paws at chest, cape hanging). The clip carries it
through the launch, so it must start from standing, not from the flying hero pose.

Settings: `animation_model: standard`, `duration: 4`, `loop: false` → **8 credits** (2/sec).
Matches the reference video's 4.064 s.

## animation_prompt

A 4-second superhero launch, one continuous shot, camera locked off, character centred in frame.

0.0–0.7s — The caped purple bat-pup stands still on the spot, both paws tucked at its chest, cape
hanging straight down behind it. It settles and breathes once, ears twitching slightly.

0.7–1.1s — Anticipation. It bends its knees and squashes downward into a deep crouch, shoulders
dropping, cape gathering in, eyes lifting to look upward.

1.1–1.3s — Launch. It springs straight up off the ground, body stretching tall. A soft puff of pale
lavender-white cartoon cloud bursts outward at its feet, where it pushed off.

1.3–2.6s — Rising fast, straight upward. One paw punches overhead into a raised fist. The mouth
opens wide into a delighted cheer, showing the two white fangs and the pink tongue. The red cape
snaps outward and flares wide to both sides like wings, rippling. Thin vertical motion streaks trail
below it. The cloud puffs fall away downward, spreading apart, shrinking and fading out. A few small
four-point sparkles pop and drift.

2.6–4.0s — It decelerates and settles into a steady midair hover, floating gently up and down. The
cape keeps waving slowly. It holds the raised fist and the open-mouthed grin, bobbing softly.

Style: flat 2D vector cartoon, thick dark purple outlines, clean flat fills, no gradients, no
shading changes, no texture, no film grain.

Keep the character exactly as the reference image: same lavender-purple fur, same red cape, same
gold medallion at the collar, same face, same proportions. Do not redesign it.

Background: completely empty and transparent. No background colour, no purple, no sky, no scenery,
no ground, no floor line, no shadow on a floor, no text, no logo, no letters, no numbers, no
watermark, no UI, no border.

Only the character itself and its own effects: the cloud puffs, the motion streaks and the sparkles.

Camera: static. No zoom, no pan, no rotation, no parallax.
