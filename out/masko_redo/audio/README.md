## What changed from the four rejected attempts

The fifth version is not a retune of the fourth. The measurement said the problem was never
tuning:

- **No bass at all.** No kick, no sub, no bassline, nothing below C5. The previous version put
  25.9 % of its energy below 300 Hz; the references put 0.1–2.1 % there. That one number was the
  whole failure.
- **No bus compression.** Crest factor is in the spec, and compression is what flattened the last
  attempt to 16.5 dB.
- **Short and dry.** 160 ms to −20 dB instead of 370.
- **Space kept.** 41 % of the clip is near silence. I had been treating that as a fault to fill.

## The cue sheet

| time | what |
|---|---|
| 0.58 s | crouch — one tick and a small G5 |
| 0.68 s | **takeoff** — C6 E6 G6, 55 ms apart, spreading as it climbs |
| 0.86 s | the top of the climb, C7 |
| 0.98–1.42 s | ascent and hover — a thin line of quiet notes, G6 E6 C6 G5 |
| 1.62–1.76 s | two low taps leading in, C5 and G5 |
| 1.85 s | **the wordmark** — C major triad struck 6 ms apart, C7 an octave over it |
| 2.30 / 2.40 s | two flecks for the sparkles |

Nine haptic events come from the same sheet, so they cannot drift: a light tap on the crouch, a
ramped continuous plus a hard transient on the takeoff, one at the top, a lead-in at 1.76, the
strongest on the wordmark with a short continuous under it, and two tiny ones on the sparkles.
The quiet ascent notes get none — they are texture, and buzzing on each would turn the clip into
a rumble.

## Two bugs worth remembering

**The synth was silent.** `if e < 0.0005: break` was tested during the attack ramp, where the
envelope legitimately starts at zero — so every pitched note broke out at sample 0 and only the
unpitched ticks survived. The measurement caught it: three cue times read as exactly 0.0000 RMS.

**The analyser lied.** It took its spectrum from the start of the loudest 400 ms window, which on
sparse material lands in the silence *before* a hit — reporting a centroid of 0 Hz. It now scans
for the loudest 4096-sample window inside that span. Worth knowing because the bug only appears on
sparse content, which is exactly the kind this spec asks for.

⚠ **Never `-c:v copy` the HEVC-alpha movie to add audio** — the alpha layer is a second parameter
set and copying corrupts it (`PPS id out of range`, frames decode opaque). Encode video and audio
in one pass; verified afterwards at 6.8 % opaque.
